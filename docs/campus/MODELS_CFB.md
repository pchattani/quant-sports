# The Quant Campus: college football models (FBS, `cfb`)

Code: `oddsmarkets/college/football/` (data, ep, ratings, game, backtest, committee, sim, players, fantasy, build).
Inputs: the season stores and play files of `oddsmarkets/college/store.py` only (ESPN scoreboards, summaries and box
scores, play-by-play, ESPN core odds, AP / Coaches / CFP rankings, rosters), read only. No Sports-Reference,
Massey, 247Sports, On3, Rivals or NCAA.com data; no recruiting rankings; no official transfer-portal data.
Every validation number below is walk-forward (no game is predicted with information from its own day or later)
and is regenerated by every build into `data/cfb/calibration.json`. Numbers here: build of 21 Oct 2026 (stores of
19 Oct 2026: 2001-2026, the 2026 season through week 8, 510 finished games).

**Headline.** Against ESPN's closing lines, 2013-2026 (11,561 games with a close), the model's margin error is
12.87 points against the close's 12.33; its side of the closing spread wins 49.7% ± 0.5 (n 11,410), i.e. no advantage,
as expected of a public-data model; on games with both moneylines its Brier score is 0.1854 against the de-vigged
market's 0.1786. The gap is largest in weeks 1-2 (13.56 v 12.52) and in games against non-FBS teams (13.90 v
12.51), and smallest from week 9 on (12.64 v 12.40). Since 22 Oct 2026 the published line stacks this ratings
model with market-implied ratings (section 3b): on the sealed 2021-2026 test it lowers log-loss by 0.0063 [0.0034,
0.0092] and closes 35 % of the gap to the closing moneyline; it still trails the close.

## 1. Expected points, EPA and success rate (`ep.py`)

- **Label**: the next score in the same half from the snapping team's point of view, one of seven classes (TD +7,
  FG +3, safety +2, none, and the opponent's three). Scoring plays are taken from ESPN's `scoring` flag (the play
  text alone misleads: "TOUCHDOWN nullified by penalty", plays under review), and who scored from the play type
  and text (offensive TDs; interception / fumble / punt-return / blocked-kick TDs for the defence; field goals;
  safeties). ESPN's running score on college plays is written per drive and is not used play by play.
- **Play types**: passes are ESPN types 3, 4, 7, 24, 26, 36, 63, 67 plus 6 ("Pass Interception", 2008-2014) and 51
  ("Pass", 2005-2007); timeouts, period ends, coin tosses, PATs and two-point tries are never snaps.
- **Model**: multinomial logistic regression (scipy L-BFGS, analytic gradient, L2 1e-3) on yards to the end zone
  (linear plus hinges at 5/10/20/35/50/65/80/90/95), down dummies and their interactions with field position and log
  distance, goal to go, seconds left in the half, a final-two-minutes ramp (and its interaction with field position)
  and the half. Overtime is excluded. Fits use at most 700,000 plays (whole games).
- **Seasons**: ESPN's plays carry field position from 2005; 2001-2004 plays have no yard line, so those seasons
  have no EP / EPA (their team ratings are points-only).
- **No season is scored by a model that saw it**: seasons 2005-2011 (no closing lines) are cross-fitted inside that
  block (round-robin folds); every later season is scored by a model fitted on the seasons before it (walk-forward,
  refitted every two seasons: 2012-13 on 2005-11, 2014-15 on 2005-13, ...), so the EPA inputs of the backtest
  against closing lines use earlier seasons only; the current season uses the model fitted on every completed season.
  12 fits in all.
- **EPA** = EP after the snap (the points when it scores, else the next snap's EP in the half, sign-flipped on a
  change of possession, else 0 at the end of the half) minus EP before.
- **Success**: 50% of the distance on 1st down, 70% on 2nd, 100% on 3rd/4th (or a touchdown); turnovers fail.
- **Garbage time** (excluded from team ratings and player value): margin above 38 in the 2nd quarter, 28 in the
  3rd, 22 in the 4th (score rebuilt from the scoring plays).

Out-of-sample fit, 2005-2025 (2,673,888 plays): multinomial log loss 1.296 against 1.490 for the class base rates;
mean absolute calibration error of EP by one-point bins 0.165 points (per season 0.12-0.25; worst 2020, the short
season). Per season log loss 1.278-1.322.

## 2. Team ratings (`ratings.py`)

Three ridge models share one solver; each observation is one team's offence in one game:

    points(t v o)   = mu + h/2 [t at home] - h/2 [o at home] + off_t - def_o     (margins beyond 40 count at 20%)
    EPA/play(t v o) = same form, weight = scrimmage snaps / 65, garbage time excluded
    success(t v o)  = same form

`def` is points (EPA, success) prevented, so a team's net rating is `off + def`. Home field `h` is estimated in
every fit (prior 2.0 points).

- **Preseason prior**: last season's final off/def regressed toward the mean of the team's *new* conference
  (last season's ratings of that conference's members): prior = conf_mean + 0.8 (last - conf_mean). FBS teams
  without a rating last season start at -8 (net). Prior strength 3 game-equivalents.
- **Non-FBS opponents** are rated as off = g_FCS + c_conf + u_t: a shared FCS level, a level for the team's FCS
  conference (carried from season to season at 0.95, precision 2 game-equivalents) and the team's own deviation
  (precision 2), which is carried from the last season the team met an FBS opponent at 0.9 per season since (an FCS
  team plays one or two FBS games a year and often skips years). This replaced one shared FCS level with no
  carry-over (the earlier build also dropped the FCS prior by mistake): walk-forward margin error on 2002-2011 fell
  from 12.93 to 12.66 (games against FCS teams 14.98 to 13.39).
- **Recency**: tuned to (practically) none within a season (half-life 2,000 days).
- **Hyperparameters** (prior strength, carry-over, margin cap, FCS treatment, new-member prior, home prior) were
  chosen by one-at-a-time sweeps on the walk-forward margin MAE of **2002-2011**, seasons with no closing lines,
  so every season in the comparison with the close is out of sample for them (re-checked 21 Oct 2026; weighting the
  season before last into the prior did not help).
- **Returning production is not used in the ratings**: the stores' past-season rosters mix seasons (2019-2025 rosters
  list 150-190 athletes per team and imply 86-96% of last season's yards return). The published line (section 3b)
  uses a box-score version: last season's players, a return model fitted on earlier seasons, and who has actually
  appeared once a team has played. It helps little (dev -0.0005 nats), and even knowing exactly who returned would add
  only 0.0002 more, so the weeks 1-2 gap is mostly something else (freshmen, transfers before they play, coaching).
- **Walk-forward**: for every game day D (US day = UTC - 8 h) the models are refitted on finished games with day
  < D (normal equations accumulated once per season, one solve per day).
- **Rating** (the number on the site) = the predictive blend below expressed per team (points v an average FBS team
  on a neutral field); `off`/`def` are the points model's components rescaled to sum to it; `epa_off`/`sr_off` are
  adjusted rates produced, `epa_def`/`sr_def` adjusted rates allowed; `pts_rating` the points model alone; `sos` the
  mean rating of opponents played.

## 3. Game model and the blend (`game.py`)

    margin_home = b0 [not neutral] + b1 d_pts + b2 d_epa + b3 d_sr + b4 d_rest
    total       = a + c x (points-model expected points of both teams)
    p_home      = Phi(margin / sd),  sd by phase (weeks 1-3, later, postseason) from the residuals

`d_*` are home-minus-away net ratings; `d_rest` the difference in days since each team's last game (capped at 14).
The weights, the totals line and the sd are refitted **before every season on earlier seasons' walk-forward rows
only** (ridge on the EPA and success columns, which are collinear with the points rating). Seasons without
play-by-play ratings use the points-only blend fitted the same way. Travel distance is not modelled (no coordinates
in the free feeds). An Elo baseline (margin-of-victory Elo, K 25, home 65, one-third regression to the conference
mean between seasons) is reported alongside.

Blend used for 2026 (fitted on 2001-2025, 17,294 games with EPA ratings): margin = 2.93 home + 0.80 d_pts + 3.36
d_epa + 30.4 d_sr + 0.07 d_rest; sd 16.7 (weeks 1-3), 16.0 (later), 16.5 (postseason); total = 20.9 + 0.634 x
points-model total.

## 3b. The published game line: market-informed (`market_informed.py`, from 22 Oct 2026)

Since 22 October 2026 the game probabilities and margins on the site are the ratings model above stacked with what
the betting market has said about each team in earlier games (the pattern that closed 30-50 % of the gap to the close
in the pro sports). Everything is known before the game day:

    mk = h [not neutral] + r_home - r_away       market ratings: weighted ridge on the closing spreads of games on
                                                 earlier days (half-life 120 days, ridge 0.1; preseason prior = 0.9 x
                                                 last season's final market rating, regressed toward its conference)
    rd = h' [not neutral] + d_home - d_away      residual ratings: the same ridge on (closing spread - our margin),
                                                 i.e. the market's persistent disagreement with our ratings (no decay,
                                                 ridge 0.25, carried into the next season)
    rp = returning production, home minus away   last season's box-score player value per game expected back (QB and
                                                 the rest; a return model fitted on earlier seasons: seasons already
                                                 played, games-played share, position, value), plus transfers in once
                                                 they have played (`returning.py`)
    margin = c + b . [home, m, mk, rd, (mk, m, rd) x early, (mk, m) x non-FBS, rp, rp x early]
    P(home) = Phi((margin + c0) / sd_phase)

`m` is the ratings model's margin; "early" means either team has fewer than two earlier games with a line this season.
The stack is fitted to closing spreads of seasons 2013 .. S-1 and the probit to those seasons' walk-forward
predictions; both are frozen per season in `oddsmarkets/college/fitted/informed_cfb.json` (2026: fitted on
2013-2025, 11,052 games; m 0.91, rd 0.43, mk 0.07 plus 0.08 early and 0.19 against non-FBS teams; sd 15.2 / 14.6 /
19.8 for weeks 1-3 / later / postseason). Totals and the season simulation still use the ratings model.

Pre-registered test (`docs/research/college_edge.md`, round 4; choices made on 2015-2020, one run on 2021-2026):
log-loss 0.4871 -> 0.4808 on 5,059 games, paired difference -0.0063 [-0.0092, -0.0034]; Brier 0.1629 -> 0.1606;
calibration slope 1.05 -> 0.98, ECE 0.011 -> 0.011. Against the de-vigged closing moneyline (4,474 games) the gap
fell from 0.0174 to 0.0113 nats (35 % closed); margin error on games with a close 12.70 -> 12.52 (the close 12.08).
The gain is largest where the ratings model was weakest: weeks 1-2 (-0.0101) and games against non-FBS teams
(-0.0132). The game page shows the ratings model, the market-ratings line and the published line side by side.

## 5. Season simulation, conference title games and the CFP (`sim.py`, `committee.py`)

- **Remaining games**: per simulation every team draws a strength shift ~ N(0, se), se = 15 / sqrt(3 + games
  played), so results stay correlated within a run; each game is margin = home + power_h - power_a + shifts + noise
  (noise sd chosen so the total matches the game model's sd). 20,000 simulations.
- **Conference standings**: conference win percentage, head-to-head wins among teams with the same percentage
  (a mini-league), then rating (a documented proxy for the remaining tiebreakers). **Title games**: the top two, or
  the division winners where the conference has divisions that season (Sun Belt); a conference plays one when the
  store holds its title game (real, or a placeholder slot matched to the conference by its headline, e.g. "Big Ten
  Championship"); neutral site.
- **Committee-ranking proxy**: a linear resume score fitted to ESPN's CFP rankings 2014-2025 (every weekly ranking
  and the selection ranking, the latter weighted 3x) by pairwise logistic regression, P(i above j) =
  sigmoid(w . (x_i - x_j)), over ranked pairs and (ranked, unranked top-60) pairs. Features: wins, losses, our
  points rating at the ranking's release, strength of schedule, wins over our top 25, losses outside our top 60
  (or to FCS teams), conference champion, power-conference member. In the sim each team's score gets Gumbel noise,
  so pairwise orders follow the fitted logistic.
- **Committee proxy accuracy** (leave one season out, each season's selection-day ranking, 12 seasons 2014-2025):
  Spearman 0.933 over the committee's top 25, top-12 overlap 90.3%, top-4 overlap 83.3%. The AP poll of the same week
  scores 0.973, 96.5% and 93.8%, but it is published alongside the committee's ranking, not before it. Fitted weights
  (standardised): wins 1.26, losses -1.34, rating 0.98, SOS 0.27, top-25 wins 0.41, bad losses -0.24, conference
  champion 0.02, power conference 0.41.
- **Formats** (`sim.FORMATS`):

| Seasons | Field | Rule | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2001-2013 | 2 | BCS title game (not simulated) | yes |
| 2014-2023 | 4 | Top four of the committee ranking; 1 v 4, 2 v 3 at bowl sites; neutral final | yes |
| 2024 | 12 | Five highest-ranked conference champions + seven others; the four highest-ranked champions seeded 1-4 with byes; first round on campus (5v12, 6v11, 7v10, 8v9) | yes |
| 2025 | 12 | Same field; seeded 1-12 straight by ranking | yes |
| 2026 | 12 | **2025 rules assumed (unverified)** | no |

- **Bowl eligibility**: six wins including a conference title game (the 5-7 rules are ignored). Bowl games are not
  simulated; `exp_w` covers the regular season and title game.
- **CFP-field sim backtest** (as of the first conference-championship day of each season 2014-2025; walk-forward
  ratings; committee proxy fitted without that season; the title games simulated even where played, so no result
  from championship weekend is used; field = the top N by P(in)): 54 of 64 playoff places named (84.4%; 4-team era
  34 of 40, 12-team era 20 of 24), Brier of P(in) over FBS teams 0.0102, mean P(in) given to the teams that made it
  0.655; mean log loss of the eventual champion's title probability 1.52 (a uniform guess over the field: 1.39 for 4
  teams, 2.48 for 12).

## 6. Players (`players.py`)

- **Attribution**: passer, target and ball carrier of every pass/rush snap are parsed from ESPN's play text (both
  the abbreviated "#11 C.Beck pass complete ... to #10 M.Toney" style and the older "Jaxson Dart pass complete to
  Cayden Lee" style) and matched to that game's box score for the offence by first initial + surname (suffixes
  dropped), then jersey number, then surname alone when unique. Matched 2020-2026: passer 97.7-98.8% of pass snaps,
  ball carrier 96.9-98.9% of rushes, target 63-86% of pass snaps (sacks and throwaways have none; 2024 lowest).
- **Value (points above replacement)**: QB = EPA of dropbacks (completions, incompletions, sacks, interceptions) +
  rushes; RB = rushes + targets; WR/TE = targets + rushes; garbage time excluded. Replacement = the EPA per play of
  the non-starters at the position (outside each team's top QB 1 / RB 2 / WR 3 / TE 1 by plays): value = EPA -
  replacement rate x plays. Defence and specialists from the box score with documented weights (solo tackle 0.25,
  assist 0.12, TFL +0.9, sack +1.0, pass defended 0.7, hurry 0.35, interception 3.5, defensive TD 4.0; kicking
  3 x (FGM - 0.78 FGA) + (XPM - 0.96 XPA); punting 0.035 x (gross average - 41.5) x punts + 0.25 per inside-20 -
  0.1 per touchback; returns 0.03 x (kick-return yards - 21 per return) + 0.05 x (punt-return yards - 8 per
  return) + 3 per return TD), replacement = the non-starters' per-game points (DL 4 / LB 3 / DB 4). Values are
  comparable within a position; QB values are larger by construction.
- **Validation** (2020-2026): season-to-season correlation of a player's value per credited play, same position,
  enough volume both seasons: QB 0.39 (n 408), RB 0.23 (668), WR 0.35 (175); value per game DL 0.51, LB 0.39, DB 0.29.
  A team's summed skill value per game correlates 0.67-0.86 with its opponent-adjusted offensive EPA rating.
- **Positions**: the roster position (any recent season's store), else inferred from usage. Players of 2001-2003
  have no names in the stores (no rosters then), so those catalogues show ids only.
- **Units** (team pages): QB, RB, WR/TE, defensive front (DL), LB, DB, ST = the top players' value per game; OL = adjusted
  rushing EPA per play as a proxy (no individual line data). Graded by FBS percentile (A >= 80, B >= 60, C >= 40,
  D >= 20, F). Strengths = the top three units, needs = the bottom three.
- **Inferred transfers**: the same athlete id on a different team from one season to the next (box score, else the
  current roster), with value before (last season) and after (this season so far). Labelled "inferred from box
  scores and rosters; not the official portal". 2026: 2,297 moves into or out of FBS teams, 1,985 of them with a
  game for the new team.
- **Suggestions** for each need: the highest per-game value at the unit's positions on other teams, with the gain
  over the team's best at the position. Labelled as model suggestions (eligibility, the window, NIL, fit and
  availability are not considered).

## 7. Fantasy (`fantasy.py`)

PPR / half / standard from the box score (passing 0.04/yd, 4/TD, -2/INT; rushing and receiving 0.1/yd, 6/TD;
receptions 1 / 0.5 / 0; fumbles lost -2; kickers 3 per FG, 1 per extra point, no distance bonus). Projection =
usage (exponentially weighted points this season, half-life 6 games, shrunk toward the position level) x opponent
((points allowed to the position / league mean, shrunk with 10 games) ^ 0.5) x game script ((implied team points
from the game model / the team's average) ^ 0.25). Parameters chosen on 2025 only.

Walk-forward backtest (every player-game with two or more earlier games that season, projected from earlier game
days only), PPR, the seasons other than 2025: 67,391 player-games 2021-2024 and 2026, mean absolute error 5.01
points against 5.13 for the naive season-to-date average; mean Spearman rank correlation within (day, position)
groups 0.534 against 0.513. The model beats the naive projection in every season (2025, the tuning season: 4.84 v
4.97).

## 8. Markets

`markets.json` sets our champion, CFP and conference-title probabilities beside Kalshi / Polymarket futures
(`oddsmarkets/college/sources/markets.py`), and per-game model v market probabilities (prediction markets, else ESPN's

## 9. Limits

- Lines exist from 2012 and 2012's fail the sign check, so the comparison with the close starts in 2013; ESPN's
  matchup predictor is not stored for finished games (the opening win-probability value stands in, 2016 on).
- No recruiting or portal data; returning production only from box scores (the stores' past rosters mix seasons).
- The market-informed line needs earlier closing lines: it is published for the current season only (past seasons'
  pages keep the ratings model), and its fit is refreshed once a season (`scripts/campus_r4.py fit`).
- Weather is not in the model: day-ahead forecasts exist free only from 2024 (round 4 test in college_edge.md).
- 2001-2004 plays have no field position: no EPA for those seasons; 2001-2003 players have no names.
- Non-FBS teams are seen only in their games against FBS teams, which caps how well they can be rated.
- The 2026 CFP format is assumed to follow the 2025 rules until confirmed.
- Player value is built from ESPN play text; names that cannot be matched to the box score earn no credit.
