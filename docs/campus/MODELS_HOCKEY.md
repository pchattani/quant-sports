# The Quant Campus: college hockey models (men's `mch`, women's `wch` Division I)

Code: `oddsmarkets/college/hockey/` (espn, data, model, npi, sim, players, build) and the source module
`oddsmarkets/college/sources/chn.py`. Every validation number below is walk-forward (no game is predicted with
information from its own day or later) and is regenerated by every build into `data/<L>/calibration.json`.

## 1. Data

- **ESPN** (public JSON): college hockey scoreboards, every day of every season from 2005-06: teams, start times,
  results, period line scores, overtime and shootout flags, venues, attendance, the postseason notes that name the
  conference tournament and NCAA rounds, and the current USCHO poll. ESPN has no box scores or play-by-play for
  college hockey. Its women's coverage is complete only from 2025-26.
- **College Hockey News** (collegehockeynews.com), credited on every hockey page, used with the site owner's
  approval: team season pages (every skater's and goalie's line, men's shot attempts by strength from 2013-14,
  every game's goals and shots by period, power plays, penalty minutes, attendance and, recently, CHN's xG),
  rosters (position, class, height, weight, birth date, hometown, last team, NHL draft) and box scores (scoring with
  assists, times and situations, penalties, goalies, skater lines with time on ice for men, faceoffs, CHN's xG).
  - **Politeness:** robots.txt obeyed; a User-Agent naming the project; one request at a time across every process
    on the PC, at least 3 s apart; back-off on 429 / 403 / 5xx; every raw page cached so nothing is fetched twice;
    fetched from one PC only (never CI). Raw pages and the parsed stores are private; the site shows derived facts
    and our own model output.
  - **robots.txt excludes box scores dated 2024 and earlier**, so for those seasons the game-level detail is the
    team game-by-game lines (goals and shots by period, power plays, penalty minutes) and the player detail is the
    season lines; scoring summaries, time on ice and primary assists exist from January 2025.
  - **No shot locations.** CHN publishes no shot coordinates in its box scores or metrics pages (checked for current
    and past games), so there is no expected-goals model of our own; CHN's own xG is shown, labelled as theirs.
  - **One switch:** `CHN_ENABLED=0` stops every CHN fetch and parse; the builder then writes every CHN-derived
    payload (players, value, moves, box scores, shot and special-teams numbers) as `ok: false` and the site runs on
    ESPN results alone.
- **Joining the two.** Team ids are ESPN's school ids (the same school id as its basketball team). Each CHN team is
  matched to ESPN by its results (date, goals for, goals against) within a season, names as the fallback; CHN-only
  games (ESPN misses a few) get their own ids. Division I membership and conferences come from CHN's per-season team
  lists. The NCAA record counts a shootout as a tie (conferences use it only for their standings point).

## 2. Ratings and the game model (`model.py`)

Per game, regulation goals of each side are Poisson:

    log E[goals_home] = mu + hfa (not on neutral ice) + att_home - def_away
    log E[goals_away] = mu + att_away - def_home

fitted by Newton steps on the decayed, ridge-penalised Poisson likelihood of every earlier game of the season
(weights 0.5^(age / half-life)), each team's attack and defence shrunk toward its prior (carry x last season's
final value; zero for a new program); teams outside Division I share one pooled pair. Regulation goals strip the
overtime winner (and shootouts) from the final score. A rating is the expected goal difference a game against an
average Division I team on neutral ice.

The outcome layer: the two Poisson means give regulation win / level / loss, with ties inflated by `rho` (hockey ends
level more often than independent Poisson goals say); a level game goes to overtime, decided with probability
`q` (five minutes 5-on-5 before 2021-22, 3-on-3 since; tournament games are played to a winner), the stronger
side more often. The record outcome is win, tie or loss.

Chosen on the men's development seasons 2006-07 .. 2023-24 (60 grid points: half-life x ridge x carry-over;
the tie and overtime layer read off the same seasons): half-life 365 days, ridge 35, carry-over 0.85, tie inflation
rho 0.251, overtime decided q = 0.423 (5-on-5, before 2021-22) and 0.613 (3-on-3). The women's model is tuned on its
own seasons the same way (below).

**Validation** (walk-forward, every game between two Division I teams): log-loss of the three outcomes against a
naive baseline (the previous season's share of home wins, ties and away wins; neutral ice split evenly). The
parameters were chosen on the development seasons only; 2024-25 and 2025-26 were sealed and scored once.

| Level | Seasons | Games | Model log-loss | Baseline | Model - baseline [95% CI] | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Men | 2006-07 .. 2023-24 (development) | 19,357 | 0.899 | 0.950 | -0.051 [-0.057, -0.044] | 58.5% |
| Men | **2024-25 + 2025-26 (sealed)** | 2,314 | **0.828** | 0.895 | **-0.067 [-0.081, -0.052]** | 60.9% |
| Men | 2024-25 | 1,167 | 0.839 | 0.913 | -0.074 [-0.094, -0.054] | 61.4% |
| Men | 2025-26 | 1,147 | 0.818 | 0.880 | -0.062 [-0.084, -0.041] | 60.3% |
| Women | 2021-22 .. 2023-24 (development) | 1,921 | 0.681 | 0.898 | -0.217 [-0.237, -0.197] | 72.7% |
| Women | **2024-25 + 2025-26 (sealed)** | 1,497 | **0.706** | 0.875 | **-0.169 [-0.194, -0.145]** | 68.7% |

Both levels' sealed seasons beat the baseline with a CI that excludes zero, so both models are promoted. The women's
parameters (half-life 700 days, ridge 18, carry-over 0.85, rho 0.177, q 0.360 / 0.615) were chosen on only three
development seasons, because CHN's women's game lines for 2017-18 .. 2020-21 were still being fetched; they will
be re-chosen once those are in. The women's game is more lopsided (the favourite wins more often), hence the lower
log-loss. Ties are slightly under-forecast for men (6.7% forecast v 7.5% seen in the sealed seasons) and close for
women (6.3% v 6.3%). Men's sealed-season calibration (home-win chance in buckets, forecast v seen): 0.21 v 0.24,
0.31 v 0.28, 0.40 v 0.40, 0.50 v 0.50, 0.60 v 0.60, 0.69 v 0.72, 0.79 v 0.76, 0.90 v 0.88.

## 3. The NCAA selection replica (`npi.py`)

- **NPI** (men's rules below; the women's NPI has no home / road weights, counts an overtime win as two thirds of
  a win and one third of a loss, and starts the quality-win bonus at 51.5): a win is worth 25 + 0.75 x the opponent's NPI plus the quality-win
  bonus 0.5 x (opponent NPI - 51) when the opponent is above 51; a loss 0.75 x the opponent's NPI. Weights: road
  wins and home losses 1.2, home wins and road losses 0.8, neutral 1.0, conference tournament games unweighted; an
  overtime win counts 0.6 of a win and 0.4 of a loss, the home / road weight on the regulation part only. The NPI is
  the weighted mean of the game values, iterated to a fixed point; wins that would lower it are dropped once a team
  has 12 weighted wins, losses that would raise it are always dropped. Non-Division-I games do not count. The bonus
  slope (0.5 a point above 51) was read off CHN's team detail page, which publishes each game's value.
- **PairWise** (to 2024-25): RPI = 0.25 WP + 0.21 OWP + 0.54 OOWP with the home / road weights from 2013-14 and
  overtime wins at two thirds from 2021-22, the quality-win bonus for wins over the RPI top 20 (0.050 down to 0.0025,
  over weighted games), bad wins dropped; every pair of teams compared on the RPI, the record against common
  opponents (mean of per-opponent percentages from 2012-13) and head to head (plus the record against teams under
  consideration before 2013-14); comparisons won rank the teams, RPI breaks ties.

Checked against College Hockey News's published tables (`scripts/cc_hockey_validate.py`, regenerated by every
build into the Model page):

- **NPI, 2026-27 to 30 Oct:** men 58 of 58 teams within 0.005 of CHN (mean difference 0.0026, the rounding of
  the published table), every rank identical; women 45 of 45 within 0.005 (mean 0.0024), every rank identical.
- **PairWise, 2005-06 .. 2025-26 (21 seasons, games before the first NCAA game):** RPI mean absolute difference
  0.0038; ranks identical 30%, within one 59%; the top 16 (the at-large picture) agrees on 15.2 of 16 teams a season
  on average (16 of 16 in 6 seasons). Before 2013-14 CHN's tables name only the teams under consideration (about 30).
  The misses are mostly adjacent teams on near-identical RPIs; 2020-21 (the COVID season, RPI difference 0.0105) is
  the worst, as the NCAA ran that season with special rules.

## 4. The season simulation (`sim.py`)

Remaining regular-season games from the game model with a per-run strength shift (the ratings' uncertainty);
conference standings on points (3-2-1-0 from 2021-22); conference tournaments generic and approximate (every member,
single elimination, byes to the top seeds, the higher seed at home before the semifinals; real formats differ and
are not in a free feed); the NPI on each simulated season (vectorised); the field (men: six automatic bids and ten
at-large; women: five and six, 11 teams from 2021-22, 8 before); men's bands of four in four regionals (1 v 16
serpentine, No. 1 overall's region meets No. 4's), women's 6v11 / 7v10 / 8v9 first round and quarterfinals at the
top four; regional hosts and the committee's first-round conference swaps are not modelled. A finished season shows
the real field with our pre-tournament chances through the actual bracket (ratings from games before the first NCAA
game), and the History tab lists our favourite against the champion every year.

## 5. Player value: GAR (`players.py`)

From CHN's season lines and, from 2025, its box scores:

- **Skaters.** Goals created, GC = (G + 0.5 A) x team goals / (team G + 0.5 team A), minus the replacement
  forward's or defenceman's GC per game (the 20th percentile of regulars) x GP; plus 0.25 x the player's +/- against
  his team's goal difference (the team's goal difference per game x GP x 5/18), shrunk by GP / (GP + 25); minus 0.17
  goals per minor penalty against the position's average; plus 0.01 goals per net faceoff win.
- **Goalies.** Goals saved above an average goalie, (SV% - league SV%) x shots, shrunk by shots / (shots + 600),
  plus the replacement margin (one point of save percentage) x shots.
- **WAR** = GAR / 6 (a team scores about three goals a game).

**Limits.** No on-ice shot data or shot locations, so the value is production-based, not play-driving: goals created
and +/- reward good linemates and power-play time; +/- is noisy and so carries a small weight. Time on ice exists
only for men from 2025; women's lines have no time on ice and no shot attempts. Read GAR within a position.

## 6. Transfers

A player keeps one CHN id across schools: the same id on a different Division I team from one season to the next is
an inferred transfer (the transfer portal is not public data). CHN's roster "last team" names a player's previous
school and is shown on the team page.

## 7. Markets

Kalshi lists the men's national title (KXNCAAH) and some men's games (KXNCAAHGAME); both are shown quietly, small
and grey beside our own number, never as a model input. Polymarket listed no college hockey markets on 30 Oct 2026;
neither lists women's college hockey.

## 8. Polls

USCHO's Division I men's and women's polls (uscho.com, credited; switch `USCHO_ENABLED=0`): every week of the
current season and the final poll of past seasons, shown on the Standings page's Polls tab and the team pages. With
the switch off, the current USCHO poll that ESPN carries is used.
