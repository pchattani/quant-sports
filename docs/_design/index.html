Accessibility

  • Text contrast: primary ink 16.9:1, secondary 8.4:1, muted 4.9:1 on the dark card; light mode is similar. Muted ink is the floor for any text.
  • Marks: team and accent colours pass through QK.color.ensure() to reach 3:1 against the surface before they are drawn.
  • Identity is never colour alone: legends on every multi-series chart, direct end labels, team abbreviations on marks, icons and words on status.
  • Keyboard: skip link, visible focus rings, tabs move with arrow keys, sortable headers are buttons, search opens with / and moves with arrows.
  • Motion: prefers-reduced-motion turns off pulses, shimmer, lifts and chart animation.
  • Charts carry role="img" and an aria-label; every headline chart sits beside a table or list with the same numbers.
  • Forced colours and print fall back to system colours and drop the sticky chrome.
